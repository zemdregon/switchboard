# Support

Switchboard is pre-release software maintained on a best-effort basis.

- **Setup problems:** run `switchboard doctor` and read the
  [troubleshooting table](docs/guide/getting-started.md#troubleshooting). Most first-run problems are a missing
  provider login or a config path that does not exist.
- **Questions and ideas:** open an issue with the "Feature or docs request" template.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the "Bug report" template. Include the `doctor` output and the failure code, and remove
  anything that looks like a credential first.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Provider terms:** whether your use complies with a provider's terms of service is your responsibility; see
  [Your accounts, your responsibility](README.md#your-accounts-your-responsibility).
